# Website copy: research behind it

Last updated: 21 November 2026. This explains where each claim on the site comes from, so anyone editing the copy can check it before changing or reusing it.

## Rule for the copy: no region

We operate from India and have no legal entity in the GCC. The site therefore names no country or region (UAE, Saudi Arabia, GCC, Gulf) and no regulator or law. It describes the general problem regulated organisations face anywhere.

Many of the sources below are Gulf regulations. They show that the problem exists, and they are the evidence we will use when we focus on a region. They must not appear on the site until we are registered there. Similar rules exist elsewhere, for example India's RBI requirement to store payment data in India.

## Who buys, and who decides

- **Buyers:** banks and insurers, hospitals and health providers, government bodies, law firms and in-house legal teams, energy and critical-infrastructure operators.
- **People in the decision:** IT (server, sign-in), security (review), compliance or legal (data rules), a business owner (use case, test questions), and procurement or finance (price).

## Style rules for the copy

- Use plain words a non-technical reader understands: "work account", not "directory"; "private, encrypted connection", not "tunnel"; always explain "on your premises".
- Describe what we do. Don't describe what others do or compare ourselves with them.
- Don't limit the offer to "one use case". Say "your use case".
- Only one statistic appears on the site (the 57% in the FAQ). Keep its source here up to date.

## Check before publishing

- **Written record of where each piece of data is stored:** the site promises it in two places. Confirm we deliver it.
- **Weekly review of answer quality in week 3:** confirm the team can do it.
- **Sign-in with work accounts set up in week 1:** confirm it's realistic for a typical client.
- **Delivery lengths (4 weeks private cloud, 8 weeks on premises):** these are our own delivery estimates, not research figures. The on-premises plan assumes the client's server is in place by week 2, and says hardware delivery comes on top. Confirm with the delivery team.
- **Pilot terms:** two weeks on a server we provide, a mutual NDA signed first, a small fee credited against the full delivery, and the server and storage destroyed afterwards with written confirmation. These are business terms we set, not research findings. Keep the NDA and deletion-certificate templates ready before offering pilots.
- **Legal entity in the footer:** add the registered Indian entity, not a GCC one.
- **Arabic:** have a native speaker review `src/i18n/ar.ts`.
