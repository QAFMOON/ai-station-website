# Product screenshots used in the hero

These are official screenshots published by open-source projects under the MIT licence. They are resized, cropped to the window and
converted to WebP. In addition, the third-party model names in the upstream demos (for example "Claude", "GPT-4.1", "claude-sonnet")
were replaced with the neutral names a real AI Station deployment shows: "Assistant" for the chat endpoint and `code-agent` for the
coding role. Nothing else in the images was changed. Replace them with screenshots of your own AI Station deployment when
you have them (keep the file names, or edit `src/components/ResultsShowcase.tsx`).
